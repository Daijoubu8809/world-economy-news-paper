# One Piece field guide for the editor

The reader is a One Piece fan. The paper's flavor setting is **medium**:

- About **one "Grand Line translation" callout per full story**, and at least half of the full stories
  should have one. Brief stories can skip it.
- **Never** use it in headlines, deks, TL;DRs, interview answers, or trade pitches. Those have to work in a
  real interview.
- An analogy has to **teach the mechanism**, not just name-drop. Ask yourself: *if they only remembered
  the One Piece version, would they understand the finance correctly?* If not, cut it.
- Use **only canon you are sure of**. This file lists the safe facts. If you're not sure a detail is
  canon, leave it out. A fan will notice a wrong fact right away, and it ruins the trust.
- Don't repeat the same analogy two runs in a row for the same story. Vary it.
- Keep it to 2–5 sentences, written like a friend who's seen every arc, not like a meme page.

## The canon you can rely on

### Geography and sailing
- **Grand Line.** The ocean belt circling the world. Its weather and currents are wildly unpredictable, and its
  islands' magnetic fields make normal compasses useless.
- **Log Pose.** A wrist compass that has to *stay on an island for a while* (from hours to days, depending
  on the island) to record its magnetic field. Only then does it point to the **next** island. It never shows the
  whole route. An **Eternal Pose** always points to one specific island.
- **Paradise** is the first half of the Grand Line. The **New World** is the far harsher second half. The **Red
  Line** is the continent splitting them. You cross it either through **Mary Geoise** (the World Government's
  capital, by permission) or **underwater via Fish-Man Island**, using a ship coated at **Sabaody Archipelago**.
- **Sabaody Archipelago.** Giant mangroves produce resin **bubbles**. A bubble coating lets a ship dive to
  Fish-Man Island, but a bad coating or a rough descent can make it pop.
- **Calm Belt.** Windless seas on both sides of the Grand Line, full of Sea Kings. Ships stall dead in the water.
- **Reverse Mountain.** The entrance to the Grand Line: the currents of four seas run *uphill* and meet.
- **Knock Up Stream** (Jaya). An explosive upward ocean geyser. It launched the Going Merry to Skypiea. If the
  timing is wrong, the ship gets destroyed.
- **Aqua Laguna** (Water 7). A giant tidal wave that hits every year. Everyone knows it's coming and still has to
  evacuate.
- **Wano.** A closed country (sakoku) with sealed borders. It was ruled by Orochi and Kaido, with
  polluting weapons factories. Kozuki Oden's dream was to open the borders.

### Powers and institutions
- **World Government (WG)**: led publicly by the **Five Elders (Gorosei)**. The secret ruler **Imu** sits on
  the "Empty Throne". The **Celestial Dragons** (World Nobles) are untouchable aristocrats.
- **Heavenly Tribute.** A heavy annual payment member kingdoms owe the WG. Kingdoms that can't pay lose its
  protection.
- **Reverie (Levely).** A summit of world kings held every four years at Mary Geoise. The last one **abolished the
  Seven Warlords system**.
- **Marines.** Ranks run Fleet Admiral → Admirals → Vice Admirals.
  - **Sengoku** was Fleet Admiral until Marineford.
  - **Akainu** (Sakazuki) has the motto **"Absolute Justice"**: wipe out any trace of evil at any cost. At
    Ohara he shelled an evacuation ship in case a scholar was aboard.
  - **Aokiji** (Kuzan) has the motto **"Lazy Justice"**: relaxed, uses discretion. He let Robin go.
  - **Kizaru** (Borsalino) has the motto **"Unclear Justice"**: hard to read, noncommittal.
  - After Marineford, **Akainu and Aokiji fought for ten days on Punk Hazard** for the Fleet Admiral seat.
    Akainu won and Aokiji left the Marines.
  - Later admirals are **Fujitora** (Issho, blind, gravity powers; he pushed to abolish the Warlords) and
    **Ryokugyu** (Aramaki).
  - **Garp** is the legendary Vice Admiral who kept turning down promotion.
- **Buster Call.** Ten Vice Admirals and a fleet of battleships ordered to wipe an island off the map (Ohara,
  Enies Lobby). This is the overwhelming-force, last-resort option.
- **Seven Warlords of the Sea.** Government-licensed pirates. When the license was revoked at Reverie, every
  former Warlord became a target overnight.
- **Four Emperors (Yonko).** The four biggest pirate powers.
  - **Three Great Powers.** Emperors, Marines and Warlords held the world in a balance of deterrence.
  - **Whitebeard's death** at Marineford left a power vacuum that the whole New World scrambled to fill.
  - After Wano, **Kaido and Big Mom fell**, and the Emperors became **Shanks, Blackbeard, Luffy and Buggy**.
    Buggy got there partly through a viral misunderstanding.
- **Cross Guild.** Buggy, Crocodile and Mihawk. They **put bounties on Marines**, flipping the bounty system around.
- **Revolutionary Army.** Led by Dragon.
- **Bounties (฿, Berries).** The WG sets them based on how *threatening* someone is, not just how strong. They
  jump after big incidents.
  - Luffy's ran 30M → 100M → 300M → 400M → 500M → 1.5B → 3B.
  - **Chopper** was famously priced at 50 and later 100 (then 1,000) Berries because the Marines
    thought he was the crew's pet. That's a textbook mispricing.
- **World Economy News Paper.** Run by **"Big News" Morgans**, a giant bird. He openly cares about news being
  *entertaining*, and he'll spin a headline to sell papers. It's delivered by **News Coos**: seagulls in caps,
  paid per paper. **Den Den Mushi** are the snail phones; Video Den Den Mushi broadcast live.
- **Haki.**
  - **Observation** senses intent. At the top level it lets you glimpse the future (Katakuri).
  - **Armament** hardens the body and lets you hit Logia users.
  - **Conqueror's** is rare. It overwhelms weaker wills.
- **Devil Fruits.** Paramecia, Zoan and Logia types. Every user loses the ability to swim. **Sea Prism Stone**
  (Kairoseki) drains a user's power on contact.
- **Blackbeard** has the Yami Yami no Mi: darkness that **pulls everything in like gravity** and cancels
  other Devil Fruit powers.
- **Poneglyphs.** Indestructible stones in an ancient script. Four **Road Poneglyphs** together point to Laugh
  Tale. **Robin** is the only Straw Hat who can read them. The WG bans reading them, and **Ohara** was
  destroyed by a Buster Call for researching the **Void Century** (100 years erased from history).
- **Gol D. Roger's execution.** One sentence ("My treasure? If you want it, I'll let you have it… I left
  everything I gathered together in one place") launched the Great Pirate Era. **Whitebeard's last words**
  ("The One Piece is real!") re-ignited it.
- **Vegapunk's Egghead broadcast.** A world-wide Den Den Mushi message revealing an inconvenient truth (the
  world is sinking). It forced everyone to rethink the state of the world.
- **Straw Hats:**
  - **Luffy** (Gear 5 / Nika)
  - **Zoro** (no sense of direction)
  - **Nami** (navigator; reads the weather from tiny signs before anyone else; once predicted a cyclone at sea from a
    shift in the air)
  - **Usopp** (tall tales that sometimes come true)
  - **Sanji**
  - **Chopper**
  - **Robin**
  - **Franky** (cola-powered shipwright)
  - **Brook**
  - **Jinbe** (helmsman)
- **Coup de Burst.** The Thousand Sunny burns barrels of cola for a huge emergency jump. It's powerful, it's
  expensive, and you can't do it all the time.
- **Arlong** charged Cocoyasi village a per-head tribute. **Crocodile** posed as a hero Warlord while
  secretly running Baroque Works, using **Dance Powder** to cause a drought and topple Arabasta.
  **Doflamingo** ran the underworld weapons and SMILE trade as the broker **"Joker."** **Big Mom**
  demanded sweets as tribute from islands under her protection.

## Pre-vetted mappings (use, adapt, or invent your own at the same quality)

| Finance idea | One Piece version | Why it actually fits |
|---|---|---|
| No forward guidance / meeting-by-meeting | **Log Pose** | It only ever points to the *next* island once it's spent time on this one. You don't get the route, just the next stop, after the data's in. |
| Data dependence, reading indicators | **Nami reading the sky** | She commits to a heading only after reading small signs others miss. That's leading indicators. |
| Extreme hawk / zero tolerance for inflation | **Akainu's Absolute Justice** | He'll accept heavy collateral damage to stamp out any trace of the threat. Overtightening risk is that same collateral damage. |
| Dove / patience | **Aokiji's Lazy Justice** | Discretion over rules. He waits and judges case by case. |
| Fedspeak that won't commit | **Kizaru's Unclear Justice** | You genuinely can't tell which way he'll move. |
| Leadership fight over policy direction | **Akainu vs. Aokiji on Punk Hazard** | Two philosophies fought over who runs the institution, and the winner sets the direction. |
| Dissents | **Fujitora breaking with the brass** | A senior official going on record against the consensus, which signals where policy may go next. |
| Shock-and-awe tightening (Volcker) | **Buster Call** | Overwhelming force to end a threat, and everything nearby gets hit too. |
| Emergency liquidity / QE | **Coup de Burst** | A huge, costly burst of fuel to escape danger. You can't run on it forever. |
| QT / draining reserves | **Blackbeard's darkness** or **Sea Prism Stone** | Something steadily pulling liquidity (power) out of the system. |
| Term premium | **Hazard pay for a longer Grand Line voyage** | The farther and longer you sail, the less predictable the weather. Crews demand more to sign on. |
| Chokepoints (Hormuz, Suez) | **Crossing the Red Line** | Only a couple of passages exist. Whoever controls them can choke trade between the seas. |
| Sanctions, tariffs, closed economies | **Wano's sealed borders** | Isolation protects the people in charge and starves everyone else. |
| Sovereign debt stress / risk premium | **A kingdom behind on its Heavenly Tribute** | Fall behind, and protection (investors' trust) disappears, so the risk price jumps. |
| Repricing after news | **A bounty jump** | The WG re-rates a threat after an incident, just as markets re-rate an asset after news. |
| Mispriced asset | **Chopper's 100-Berry bounty** | The market has the wrong model of what it's looking at. |
| Narrative / momentum over fundamentals | **Buggy becoming an Emperor** | Reputation snowballed from a misunderstanding, and the market priced the story. |
| Market-moving speech ("whatever it takes") | **Roger's execution speech** | One sentence changed every player's incentives overnight. |
| Bombshell disclosure that resets models | **Vegapunk's broadcast** | Everyone had to update their view of the world at once. |
| Bubble | **Sabaody bubble coating** | It lets you go deeper than you could otherwise, until it pops under pressure. |
| Seasonal / scheduled risk (refunding, tax day, expiry) | **Aqua Laguna** | Everyone knows it's coming, and it still floods the streets. |
| Explosive squeeze / gap move | **Knock Up Stream** | Violent and sudden. Your timing has to be perfect or you're wrecked. |
| Policy summit / election that rewrites rules | **Reverie** | Every four years, the kings meet and can abolish a whole system. |
| Power vacuum after a dominant player exits | **After Whitebeard fell** | Everyone repositions at once, and volatility spikes. |
| Protection racket / rent extraction | **Big Mom's tribute** | Pay up or lose protection. |
| Verbal FX intervention vs. actual intervention | **Bounty posters vs. sending an Admiral** | Warnings first. The real force comes only if the warnings get ignored. |
